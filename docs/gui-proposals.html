QuasselGlow — three proposals for a new GUI

All three share a calmer visual system: flat surfaces, one interactive accent, 6–7 px radius, and spacing driven by typography instead of cards and pills. The difference is where buffers, nick lists and settings live.

A – Quassel Classic, modernised safest

Dense three-column layout: network/buffer tree, flat message log, nick list. Status as a single line. Connection and settings move into dialogs. Familiar to anyone who has used Quassel.
