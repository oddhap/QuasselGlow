QuasselGlow — icon proposals

Six directions for a new application icon. Every concept is shown as a macOS-style squircle, a size ladder down to 16 px, light/dark variants, and a monochrome template for the menu bar and tray. The current icon is included for comparison at the bottom of the page.

The icon must stay readable at 16 px and must not depend on colour alone.

1Hash / channel

A bold channel sign. Instantly reads as IRC, scales perfectly to 16 px, and carries no colour dependency.

Strongest: unambiguous, timeless and the safest store/ dock icon. Risk: many IRC clients already use a hash, so it is recognisable but not distinctive.

2Chat bubble

Quassel lineage without copying its logo: a single speech bubble that reads as a conversation at any size.

Strongest: warm and approachable, clearly a chat app. Risk: the most common icon metaphor in messaging, so it needs the accent colour to feel like QuasselGlow.

3Link / glow node

The current constellation, reduced to two nodes and given a halo on the far node. Same idea, far clearer.

Strongest: keeps continuity with the existing icon while fixing its biggest weakness — the halo communicates "Glow". Risk: connection/network marks are also generic.

4Terminal prompt

A shell prompt in a dark key. Speaks to the developer/IRC-core audience and looks sharp in a dark dock.

Strongest: distinct from every other chat icon and very legible when small. Risk: reads as "terminal", not "chat", so it may undersell the client.

5Beacon / glow

A lit point with radiating rings — "connected and alive". Purest expression of the Glow name.

Strongest: unique, calm and clearly "Glow"; works beautifully as a monochrome tray icon. Risk: says nothing about IRC or chat on its own.

6Q monogram

A geometric Q whose tail doubles as a chat pointer, with a dot for a node. Brand-led rather than category-led.

Strongest: ownable and clearly branded. Risk: a letter mark depends on people already knowing the app, and a Q with a tail can read as a magnifier at 16 px.

Current icon, for comparison. A beige circle with three nodes and thin connecting lines. It is abstract, has no chat or IRC signal, uses a full-bleed circle instead of a macOS squircle, and the thin strokes disappear below 32 px. Concept 3 is the direct evolution; concepts 1, 2, 4 and 5 are cleaner breaks.

What each concept needs before shipping

1. A macOS squircle master (512 px) plus a full-size PNG ladder: 16 32 64 128 256 512.

2. A monochrome template variant for the macOS menu bar and the Windows/Linux tray, since those are drawn in a single system colour.

3. A 16 px pass with hand-tuned strokes — small sizes usually need thicker glyphs than a straight downscale.

4. A favicon/32 px and the existing quassel.ico regenerated from the chosen master.
